What it does

Play by feel.

Big chord buttons with built-in voice leading, a voicing slider, and a keyboard that lights up to show what's playing. It just sounds good.

Chords, not theory

Tap a quality, drag the voicing to open or tighten it. Every change is voice-led, so it always transitions smoothly.

Shape your sound

Warm synth voices — Rhodes, pads, bells, organ — plus a sampler. Bass patterns, drums with swing, reverb and chorus to taste.

Record & share

Lay down a four-bar loop, then export a share video with reactive ink-wash visuals that bloom with every chord. Save to Photos or send it anywhere.

Calm by design

A reactive Chinese-ink background that moves with your music, a quiet dark interface, and a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Eight packs, eight moods

A complete kit, every time.

Each pack sets the synth, bass, drums, swing, tempo, chord palette and colour. Pick a mood and start — you can change it any time.
