Privacy Policy.

Last updated · 2 July 2026

The short version: Matcha does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no third-party SDKs. Everything you make stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Matcha does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. We have no servers that receive your data, and the app makes no analytics or advertising network calls.

What stays on your device

To work as an instrument, Matcha stores the following locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your loops — the four-bar loops you record, along with their pack, key and tempo.
  • Your settings — preferences such as the selected pack, sound choices, haptics and background options (stored in the app's local settings).

You can remove all of this at any time by deleting a loop inside the app, or by deleting the app from your device.

Photo library

When you choose to export a share video, Matcha asks for permission to add that video to your photo library. This permission is used only to save videos you explicitly export. Matcha does not read, browse, or access your existing photos or videos.

No tracking

Matcha does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no advertising or analytics identifiers. In Apple's App Privacy terms, our data collection is declared as Data Not Collected and tracking as No.

Children

Matcha is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because it collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age.

Data retention & deletion

Because Matcha stores everything locally and holds no data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If Matcha ever adds a feature that changes how information is handled (for example, optional cloud sync), we will update this page and the "last updated" date above before that feature ships.
