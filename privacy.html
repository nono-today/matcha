Privacy Policy.

Last updated · 30 July 2026

The short version: Matcha does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party SDKs. Everything you make stays on your device, and the one purchase in the app is handled by Apple, not by us.

What we collect

Nothing. Matcha does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. There are no servers that receive your data, and the app makes no analytics or advertising network calls.

What stays on your device

To work as an instrument, Matcha keeps the following locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your loops — the four-bar loops you record, along with their pack, key and tempo.
  • Your songs — the arrangements you build in Studio, stored the same way as loops.
  • Your settings — preferences such as the selected pack, sound choices, haptics and background options, stored in the app's local settings.
  • Whether Studio is unlocked — a single yes/no flag, kept in a local app group so the AUv3 plugin can read it while running inside another app. It contains no receipt, no account and nothing identifying.

You can remove all of it at any time by deleting a loop inside the app, or by deleting the app from your device.

Purchases

Matcha Studio is a one-time purchase. It is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your name, email, payment details or Apple Account. Matcha asks Apple whether this device is entitled to Studio and stores only the yes/no answer locally; nothing about the purchase is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account to create.

Photo library

When you choose to export a video, Matcha asks for permission to add that video to your photo library. This permission is used only to save videos you export yourself. Matcha does not read, browse or access your existing photos or videos.

No tracking

Matcha does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no advertising or analytics identifiers. In Apple's App Privacy terms, its data collection is declared as Data Not Collected and tracking as No.

Children

Matcha is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because it collects no data at all, it is safe for people of any age.

Data retention and deletion

Because Matcha keeps everything locally and holds no data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If Matcha ever adds a feature that changes how information is handled — for example, optional cloud sync — we will update this page and the date above before that feature ships.
