Frequently asked

How do I record a loop?
Press play so the drums and bass come in, then tap the red circle. It captures your next four bars. Tap it again to stop.
Where are my saved loops?
Open the library (the top-left icon on the jam screen). Loops are stored locally on your device — the pack, key and tempo are remembered with each one.
How do I share what I made?
From a saved loop you can export a share video with the reactive ink-wash visuals, then save it to Photos or send it anywhere. Matcha asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save a video.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no cloud. Matcha works entirely on your device.
Do my loops sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Not in this version. Loops are stored on each device individually. Cross-device sync may come in a later update.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad running iOS / iPadOS 17 or later.
Is my data private?
Yes — Matcha collects no data and includes no tracking or analytics. See our Privacy Policy for details.
